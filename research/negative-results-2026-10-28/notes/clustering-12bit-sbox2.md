# Second S-box check (toy 12-bit, no cipher or full-width claim)
Question: is the weak-mixing clustering gap seen with S1 = x^3 over GF(8) (APN, max DP 1/4) general for a second 3-bit S-box?
S2 = [0,3,6,2,7,1,4,5] (bijective, max DP 1/2, six table entries equal to 4/8; picked as one random non-APN permutation, not optimised). Same model: 4 S-boxes, 12-bit state, independent keys, bit-permutation vs random-matrix layer, seeds 0-4, r=1-8, all 4095 sources, exact. Command: clus4.py 0,3,6,2,7,1,4,5 4 <layer> 8 <seed> all. Sanity: clus4 with the x^3 table reproduces the clus3 value (bitperm s0 r=4 maxEDP 1.953e-2). Files: clus4.py, out4.txt, table12_sbox2.txt. Dense equivalence was not re-run for S2 (the method does not depend on the table).
Definitions: maxEDP and maxTrail are two global maxima, possibly at different (input,output) pairs, so their ratio is not a same-pair clustering factor. 'Exact' = exhaustive over all sources, matrix-free float64, independent-uniform-round-key model; not symbolic and not a fixed-key cipher probability. The 4x-floor filter is explicit; later-round unfiltered ratios exist and are simply excluded from the above-margin summary.
Observed (floor 1/4095; ratio reported only when trail >= 4 x floor):
- Bit-permutation layer, S2: EDP/best-trail is 1.00-1.25 through r=4 (the 1.25 is seed 2 at r=4), 1.00-2.02 at r=5, 1.01-3.61 at r=6, 1.02-4.60 at r=7 (4 of 5 seeds above margin), 1.04-1.65 at r=8 (3 of 5 above margin). With S1 the same layer gave 5-6.25 at r=4 and 7.55-14.25 at r=5. So at matched rounds the S1 gap is NOT reproduced; for S2 the best trail already explains EDP closely until r=5-6.
- Random layer, S2: ratio 1.00 at r<=2, up to 2.25 at r=3, 1.06-1.30 at r=4 (3 of 5 seeds above margin). EDP is 1.8-3.1 x floor at r=5 and about 1.0-1.3 x floor from r=6 (below margin; concentration over uniform only).
- S2 shows larger observed maximum key-averaged differential probabilities than S1 in these toy cases: e.g. bitperm r=4 maxEDP 6.25e-2 vs 1.95e-2 for S1; r=8 4.1e-3 vs 1.1e-3 at S1 seed 0.
Conclusion (narrow): at matched r=4 and r=5, one S2 does not replicate the S1 gap. This is not a theorem across S-boxes and does not show clustering is absent. It is one counterexample to generalising from S1. Cause not investigated (hypothesis only: with a higher-DP S-box a single dominant trail carries most of the probability, leaving little room for clustering above it).
Limits: two S-boxes, 9/12-bit widths, 5 seeds per layer, independent keys, no linear/algebraic analysis. Observed, not proved.

Independent validation: DDT and 6-bit dense vs matrix-free zero error; the 12-bit campaign was not independently rerun.
